Information and enquiries

The website introduces the available care pathways. Its content does not replace an individual clinical assessment. Sending an enquiry or consultation request asks Team Osteon to contact you; it does not create a confirmed medical appointment.

No payment is taken through the website enquiry forms. The advertised Bimax + Genioplasty package price is €14,000. Prices marked “Starting from” indicate a starting price; prices marked “per tooth” or “per arch” apply to that unit. Your suitability, treatment scope, included services and any additional costs will be set out in an individual proposal before you agree to care.

Sending a consultation or membership enquiry does not create a paid booking or subscription. Where a paid service is arranged separately, its provider, payment requirements and cancellation or refund terms must be supplied before you commit. Telephone details are shared after consultation payment is confirmed.

Clinical care

Treatment suitability, the care provider, scope of care and the individual treatment plan need to be established separately with the clinical team.

Before-and-after photographs show individual cases. Outcomes vary and the images do not guarantee a particular result. The website and enquiry forms are not an emergency service; contact local emergency services if you need urgent help.
