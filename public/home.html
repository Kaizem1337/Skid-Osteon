MARPE + Maxillary Protraction Therapy

Starting from €3,000

A comprehensive treatment combining Miniscrew-Assisted Rapid Palatal Expansion (MARPE) with maxillary protraction to address transverse and anteroposterior maxillary deficiencies.
The treatment encompasses:

- MARPE placement and controlled skeletal expansion
- Maxillary protraction therapy through reverse-pull headgear (facemask), when clinically indicated
- Clinical and radiographic monitoring throughout treatment
Goals: Improve maxillary width, arch coordination, and the skeletal relationship between the upper and lower jaws, with treatment tailored to each patient’s individual needs..

05

MSDO

Starting from €7,000

Mandibular Symphyseal Distraction Osteogenesis
A surgical osteotomy splits the mandible through the symphysis, and a distraction device gradually pulls the two segments apart. The controlled tension stimulates new bone formation in the gap. It primarily widens the anterior mandible and mandibular dental arch, creating additional transverse space for the lower teeth.

Sagittal Maxillary Distraction Osteogenesis

Starting from €7,000

A maxillary osteotomy is performed, and a distraction device gradually advances the maxilla forward. The controlled separation stimulates new bone formation in the distraction gap, producing anteroposterior advancement of the maxilla and correcting maxillary retrusion.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions
before you begin.

Everything you need to know about surgical planning, international travel, and how we coordinate your care.

Do I need an orthodontist in my home country before reaching out?

No. Many patients reach out to Osteon before any orthodontic work has begun. If you already have an orthodontist, we collaborate directly with them to align pre-surgical preparation. If not, we help you structure the correct sequence from day one.

What is the difference between custom PEEK implants and titanium?

Titanium hardware is the gold standard for skeletal fixation during jaw repositioning. Custom PEEK (Polyetheretherketone) implants are biocompatible, 3D-milled structures tailored specifically to your anatomy for custom contouring and volume where natural bone structure requires augmentation.

How long do I need to stay in Iași for surgery?

Patients typically stay in Iași for 7 to 9 days. This includes pre-operative planning and final records, 1 to 2 nights in the hospital suite, and several days of monitored recovery before a post-operative clinical review and fit-to-fly clearance.

How do I ask about follow-up care after returning home?

Contact Team Osteon to discuss follow-up appointments and questions about your treatment plan. The team can explain the next steps for your individual case.

What is covered in the €10 consultation?

We review your photos and any existing dental scans (OPG/CBCT), discuss clinical feasibility for the desired treatment, and map out your next surgical and orthodontic milestones.
